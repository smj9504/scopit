## Who can be listed

Only individuals or firms holding a current public adjuster license in Virginia, Maryland, or the District of Columbia. You must enter each license number, state, and expiration date yourself, and they must be accurate.

## The listing is free

There is no fee to apply, to be listed, or to receive messages. Scopit will not ask for or accept any payment from you in connection with the directory, including per-lead, per-inquiry, success, referral, or ranking fees. There is no paid placement: listings appear in alphabetical order. If anyone asks you to pay for a Scopit Claims listing, it is not us.

## How we verify licenses

Before a listing goes live, we check each license against the state's official license lookup. Your profile shows each license with the date we verified it. If a license expires, it stops showing, and if no current verified license remains, the listing is hidden. Tell us when a license is renewed, changes, lapses, or is suspended, so we can check it again.

## Messages from homeowners

Homeowners choose to contact you. Each message is sent only to the adjusters the homeowner selected, with the homeowner's email as the reply-to address. Use the homeowner's information only to respond to that message. Do not add homeowners to a mailing list, share their information, or contact them for unrelated purposes.

## Following your state's rules

You are responsible for following the laws and regulations that apply to public adjusters in each state where you work, including licensing, solicitation, contract, disclosure, fee, and cancellation requirements. A message through this site does not change those obligations.

## Your profile

Your profile must be accurate and must not promise results, quote settlement amounts, or include testimonials. Scopit does not publish ratings or reviews. We may shorten or edit a profile for accuracy and length, and may decline or remove any listing.

## How you may describe the listing

You may say you are "listed in the Scopit Claims directory." Please do not say or imply that Scopit recommends, certifies, endorses, or partners with you.

## Independence

You are independent of Scopit. Scopit is not a party to any contract between you and a homeowner, does not take part in claims, and does not receive any share of your fees.

## Removal

You can ask us to remove your listing at any time. We may remove a listing if a license cannot be verified, expires, or is subject to discipline, if information is inaccurate, or if these terms are not followed.

## Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. If the change is significant, we will email listed adjusters. The date at the top of this page shows the last change.
