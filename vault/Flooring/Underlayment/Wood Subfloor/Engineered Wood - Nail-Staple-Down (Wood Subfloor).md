# Engineered Wood — Nail / Staple-Down (Wood Subfloor)

> [!info] Situation
> Engineered wood nailed or stapled down over a plywood / OSB subfloor.

## Requirement
NWFA installation guidelines and manufacturer specifications require **15 lb
asphalt felt or building paper** as a **vapor retarder and slip sheet** between
the subfloor and the flooring.

## Why it's required
This layer prevents **subfloor moisture transfer** and **eliminates squeak at the
fastener plane**. It is a standard, warranty-required component of a nail-down
installation.

## Estimating note
Standard component of the nail-down assembly — include the felt / building paper
line.
