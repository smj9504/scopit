# Solid Hardwood — Nail-Down (Wood Subfloor)

> [!info] Situation
> Solid hardwood nailed down over a plywood / OSB subfloor.

## Requirement
**Rosin paper or 15 lb felt** is required beneath nail-down solid hardwood as a
**moisture retarder** and to **prevent subfloor-to-plank friction noise**.

## Why it's required
The original layer was **removed with the damaged flooring and cannot be
reused**.

## Estimating note
Replacement is necessary for a **code- and spec-compliant installation** —
include the rosin paper / felt line.
