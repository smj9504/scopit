# Engineered Wood — Floating (Wood Subfloor)

> [!info] Situation
> Floating engineered wood over a plywood / OSB subfloor.

## Requirement
Floating installation over a wood subfloor requires an **acoustic underlayment
pad** to control deflection noise and allow proper expansion movement.

## Why it's required
Manufacturer warranty is **conditioned on the specified pad being installed**.

## Estimating note
Include the acoustic pad line — it reflects the warranty requirement.
