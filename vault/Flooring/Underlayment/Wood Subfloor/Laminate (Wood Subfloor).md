# Laminate (Wood Subfloor)

> [!info] Situation
> Laminate flooring over a plywood / OSB subfloor.

## Requirement
**Foam underlayment** is required beneath laminate over wood subfloor to provide
the manufacturer-specified **cushion and sound attenuation**.

## Why it's required
Direct installation on plywood or OSB causes **locking-joint stress and premature
seam separation**.

## Estimating note
Required component under the product warranty — include the foam underlayment
line.

- Manufacturer installation specification (foam underlayment)
