# Carpet (Wood Subfloor)

> [!info] Situation
> Carpet with cushion over a plywood / OSB subfloor.

## Requirement
Carpet cushion is a **required component of any carpet installation** and is
**not included in the carpet material line**.

## Why it's required
The existing pad **absorbed water and was removed as non-salvageable during
mitigation**.

## Estimating note
Replacement pad is required to **restore pre-loss condition** — carry it as a
separate line from the carpet material.

## Standards / references
- Carpet cushion / manufacturer spec (cushion required, separate line item)
