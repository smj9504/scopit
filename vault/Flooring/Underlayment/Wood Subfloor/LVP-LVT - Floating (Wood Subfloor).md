# LVP / LVT — Floating (Wood Subfloor)

> [!info] Situation
> Floating luxury vinyl plank / tile over a plywood / OSB subfloor.

## Requirement
Floating LVP over a wood subfloor requires the **manufacturer-specified
underlayment** — **unless a pad is factory-attached to the plank**.

## Why it's required
Underlayment **thickness is limited by product spec**; an incorrect or omitted
pad voids the warranty.

## Estimating note
> [!warning] Conditional line
> Omit the separate underlayment line where the plank has a **factory-attached
> pad**. Otherwise include the manufacturer-specified underlayment.

## Standards / references
- Manufacturer installation specification (underlayment type/thickness)
