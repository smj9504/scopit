# Carpet (Concrete Slab)

> [!info] Situation
> Carpet with pad over a concrete slab.

## Requirement
Carpet pad over concrete requires a **moisture barrier layer** to prevent slab
vapor from saturating the cushion.

## Why it's required
Without the barrier, slab vapor saturates the cushion. The existing pad and
barrier are **non-salvageable and were removed during mitigation**.

## Estimating note
Replacement is required to **return the assembly to pre-loss condition** —
include both the moisture barrier and the pad.

- Manufacturer / cushion spec (moisture barrier under pad over concrete)
