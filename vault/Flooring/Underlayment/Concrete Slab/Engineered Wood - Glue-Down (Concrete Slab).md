# Engineered Wood — Glue-Down (Concrete Slab)

> [!info] Situation
> Engineered wood glued directly down over a concrete slab.

## Requirement
Direct glue-down over concrete requires a **moisture-control membrane or
moisture-mitigating adhesive system rated to the slab's measured RH level**.

## Why it's required
Slab moisture testing per **ASTM F2170** governs the required system. The
existing barrier is destroyed during tear-out, so it cannot be reused.

## Estimating note
This line item **restores the pre-loss moisture protection assembly**. The
required system is dictated by the measured slab RH — test first, then spec.

## Standards / references
- ASTM F2170 (in-situ relative humidity in concrete slabs)
- Manufacturer moisture-mitigating adhesive / membrane spec rated to slab RH
