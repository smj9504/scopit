# LVP / LVT — Floating (Concrete Slab)

> [!info] Situation
> Floating luxury vinyl plank / tile over a concrete slab.

## Requirement
Floating luxury vinyl over concrete requires an **underlayment pad and vapor
barrier** — **unless the product carries a factory-attached pad**.

## Why it's required
Pad selection is **dictated by the manufacturer**; substituting or omitting it
voids warranty coverage.

## Estimating note
> [!warning] Conditional line
> Where the product includes an **attached pad**, only the **vapor barrier**
> line applies (drop the separate pad line).

## Standards / references
- Manufacturer installation specification (pad + vapor barrier, or attached-pad note)
