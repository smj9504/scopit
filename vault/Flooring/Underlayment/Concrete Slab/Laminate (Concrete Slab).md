# Laminate (Concrete Slab)

> [!info] Situation
> Laminate flooring over a concrete slab.

## Requirement
Laminate flooring over concrete requires a **6-mil vapor barrier plus foam
underlayment** per manufacturer specification.

## Why it's required
The **fiberboard core is not moisture tolerant**; installation without the
barrier assembly will cause **edge swelling and joint failure**.

## Estimating note
Underlayment is a **mandatory warranty condition, not an upgrade**. Include both
the vapor barrier and the foam underlayment.

## Standards / references
- Manufacturer installation specification (6-mil vapor barrier + foam underlayment)
