# Engineered Wood — Floating (Concrete Slab)

> [!info] Situation
> Floating engineered wood installed over an on-grade / below-grade concrete slab.

## Requirement
Manufacturer installation specifications require a **vapor retarder and acoustic
pad** beneath all floating engineered wood applications over concrete.
Underlayment is included as a **required component of a warrantable
installation**.

## Why it's required
Installation without this assembly **voids the product warranty** and exposes the
substrate moisture to the wear layer, resulting in **cupping and delamination**.

## Estimating note
Not an upgrade — a required component of the warrantable assembly. Include the
underlayment line with the floating engineered wood.

## Standards / references
- Manufacturer installation specification (vapor retarder + acoustic pad)
